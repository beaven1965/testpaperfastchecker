Questions teachers ask

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the app in Chrome on your phone. You can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.

What if a learner shades two answers?

TestPaper FastChecker marks it orange and asks you. Tap the answer you see on the paper, or leave it wrong.

How many items can a test have?

Up to 60 items, with choices A to D or A to E, on one sheet.

My printer is old. Will it still work?

Yes. Any black-and-white printer works. Print at 100% size, not "fit to page".

Is my learners' information safe?

Names and scores are kept only on your phone. They are not sent to any server.

How does the load work?

₱99 gives you 300 papers. The load ends when the 300 papers are used or after 60 days, whichever comes first. Unused papers are forfeited after 60 days. Loads add up, and the app shows how many papers and days you have left.

Do I have to type my learners' names?

No. Load the SF1 Excel file from your adviser and the app reads the LRN and names. You can also paste them from Excel.
